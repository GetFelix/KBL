Org and team writes are conditional on the offset they expect to land at, so a tracker that lost its lease while paused can no longer append after the new holder's records. After a refusal the tracker reads the log again and writes nothing more until Felix renews the lease by version after that read, which also holds after taking the lease at start or again once it lapsed; the next offset comes from the read, never from the refusal. An org write also gives up with 503 once the lease it started on runs short, instead of holding up the renewal (#242).
