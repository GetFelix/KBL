Ingest keeps within a request budget. A worker spends half of the vendor's reported hourly limit by default (`budget_share`, or `budget_per_hour`), keeps a fifth of it for write-backs, and sends no request to a vendor that has asked it to wait (GitHub, Jira, Azure DevOps and GitLab rate limit headers and answers), up to an hour. `poll_seconds` is now a minimum and defaults to 300, and the gap stretches to fit the budget. `reconcile_every` is replaced by `reconcile_hours` (default 24): each poll re-reads its share of the open imported issues instead of all of them every few polls. `kbl-ingest check` reports the vendor's remaining budget and the projected requests an hour.
