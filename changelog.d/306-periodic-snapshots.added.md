The running tracker now saves a fold snapshot every `KBL_TRACKER_SNAPSHOT_EVERY` records (2,000 by default), on a blocking thread from a copy taken at a record boundary, extending the hash chain from the last save instead of hashing the whole log. Before, snapshots were written only by a rebuild, so a tracker that stayed up never refreshed them. A rebuild still checks every file against the log and skips one that does not match, and a save after retention trimmed the log is no longer pruned for its low record count.
