A lease put that landed in Felix with its reply lost no longer fences the tracker that holds the lease. On a refused or unanswered lease put, the tracker reads the lease entry and, when it names this tracker's random holder token, adopts its version and renews by it; with its view of the lease already run out it treats the entry as a retake and reads the log again before writing. This covers team and org renewals, retakes, and the lease taken at start (#244).
