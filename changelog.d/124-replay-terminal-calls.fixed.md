A `complete`, `release`, `give_up` or prerequisite post retried after its record landed (the tracker died before answering, or the answer was lost) is now answered with what was written, `replayed: true` and the record's offset, instead of `lost`. `kbl-mcp` sends the claim's offset (`claim`) and `attempt` with these calls; a retry is replayed only for that claim and a record the agent wrote itself, so a claim the tracker released as lapsed is still `lost`, and a retry never ends a newer claim the agent took on the same attempt. The `claimed` answer and the terminal answers now carry the record's `offset`.
