Related items (format 18): a soft link between two items that never holds either back, unlike a dependency. A person relates items with `POST /v1/work/relate` or the `relate_items` tool and removes it with `/v1/work/unrelate` or `unrelate_items`; both cards and the item panel show it. A triage answer may name `related` items next to `depends_on`, and the `work_triage` tool says when to use which. Raise the format to 18 after upgrading (docs/upgrades.md); the golden `wasm/golden/format_18.jsonl` and `wasm/golden/format_18.state.json` pin the new fold.
