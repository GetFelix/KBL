The tracker, `kbl-ingest` and `kbl-mcp` serve Prometheus metrics on a port of their own, off until `--metrics-listen` (`KBL_TRACKER_METRICS_LISTEN`, `KBL_INGEST_METRICS_LISTEN`, `KBL_MCP_METRICS_LISTEN`) is set: request count and latency per route and status, claim latency and counts, completions, releases and give-ups, fold lag against the Felix stream, ingest poll results, errors and lag per source, and MCP tool calls, latency and errors. A new `kbl-metrics` crate holds the shared recorder and listener. Labels hold route templates, tool names and result words, never item text, names or tokens. See docs/metrics.md. Traces, an SLO page and runbooks are still to come ([#127](https://github.com/GetFelix/KBL/issues/127)).
