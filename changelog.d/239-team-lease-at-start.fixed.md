A tracker with several teams renews each team's writer lease as soon as the team starts serving, instead of a third of a lease later. A lease that lapsed (ran out without a renewal) while later teams started is renewed if Felix still has it and taken again otherwise, and the team rebuilds its state from the log before writing. Before, the renewal of a lapsed lease was refused, the team stopped serving for good, and with one team the process exited. The org lease renews the same way, so a renewal just past its expiry no longer refuses org writes for a third of a lease (#239).
