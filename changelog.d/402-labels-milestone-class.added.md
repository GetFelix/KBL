An item now carries its upstream issue's labels and milestone from import, and reconcile keeps them current. Triage sets a type, area and size from a per-team vocabulary in the standing rules (a value outside it is refused), and a person can override it with `classify_item`. Ingest rules can map `milestone_tiers`, `label_types` and `label_areas`. `work_next` and `work_peek` filter by `milestone`, `labels` (any of, without regard to case), `item_type`, `area` and `size`, and the dashboard groups and shows them. This is format 19 (docs/upgrades.md). The golden `format_19` stores the fold result of a log that imports with labels, edits them, sets a vocabulary, classifies and overrides.
