People can read the tracker over REST with their own token: `GET /v1/work/items` (optionally `?stage=`), `GET /v1/work/items/{item}` and `GET /v1/work/items/{item}/history`, which pages through the records about one item. `POST /v1/work/flag` and `/v1/work/unflag` move an item into or out of Needs you with a new `flagged` record, the first record of format 7: the tracker refuses it until a person raises each team's log to format 7 (`format_raised`, see docs/upgrades.md), after every tracker and dashboard page reading that log is this build or newer. Logs that never raise fold as before.
