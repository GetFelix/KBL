Delegated worker identities (format 21). A supervisor's agent token registers workers with `POST /v1/work/workers`; each gets its own token, acts with the supervisor's grants under per-supervisor and per-team caps, and every record it writes names the worker and its supervisor (`sup`). Worker names are `<supervisor>-<suffix>`, so they never collide with an agent's, and a worker is bound to its supervisor's token. Stopping a worker revokes its token; revoking or removing its supervisor stops it and releases its hold at no cost to the item's tries. New golden logs: `core/tests/work_format_21.jsonl` and `wasm/golden/format_21` (`format_21.jsonl`, `format_21.state.json`).
