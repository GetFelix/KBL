The quick start is easier to drive. `dev/ingest.mjs` takes `--milestone`, `--labels`, `--exclude-label`, `--cap`, `--mode admit|propose` and `--keep-config`. `dev/logs.sh` and `dev/ps.sh` show the stack's logs and containers. The tracker answers `GET /` (no token) with its name, version and `/healthz`. `connect.mjs`, `agents.mjs` and `ingest.mjs` also look in `~/.kbl/bin` and `dev/state/bin`. The dashboard keeps the gateway session and the person sign-in across a reload in that tab's sessionStorage, shows "Viewing (read-only)" with "Sign in to act", and the quick start prints a "view link" and a "person token for actions". The README has a Troubleshooting section.
