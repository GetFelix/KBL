`kbl-tool work metrics` prints the Metrics tab's numbers for a window, labelled and as JSON or CSV, from a read of `work.events` and the dashboard's own fold; it writes nothing. [docs/design/supervision-experiment.md](docs/design/supervision-experiment.md) is the protocol for the supervision experiment: hypothesis, arms, assignment, measures, sample size, threats and how results are published, including a null result.
