A version tag now also publishes `ghcr.io/getfelix/kbl-tracker` (with `kbl-ingest`) and `ghcr.io/getfelix/kbl-dashboard` for amd64 and arm64, with build provenance attestations; a manual dry run builds them and pushes nothing. A Helm chart in `deploy/helm/kbl` installs the tracker (one replica), the dashboard and optionally ingest with non-root, read-only, no-capability pods, secrets only from Secrets, and optional NetworkPolicy and Ingress. Felix is not in the chart. CI lints and renders the chart. See docs/deploy.md ([#128](https://github.com/GetFelix/KBL/issues/128)).
