Ingest now writes `unlinked` when a blocked-by or sub-issue dependency is removed upstream, so the item leaves Blocked. It removes only an edge its own `linked` created: an edge from an agent's prerequisite, from an answer that moves the work, or from a person's or an agent's link stays, and the tracker refuses ingest's `linked` and `unlinked` for it. An agent's prerequisite that names an edge ingest made first takes it over, and ingest does not retry a link refused as someone else's on every touch. A failed, rate-limited or partial listing unlinks nothing, and neither does a dependency that was transferred or is in a repo ingest cannot read.
