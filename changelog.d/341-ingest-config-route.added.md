Org format 2: an org admin raises `org.events` with `format_raised`, then sets a team's connections (`connection_set`: worker group, budget pool and served sources) and pool budgets (`budget_set`) through `POST /v1/org/records`. Ingest workers read their group's connection and budget share from the `GET /v1/ingest/config` long poll (`ETag`, `If-None-Match`, `wait` up to 60 s). A worker whose group has no connection stays file-managed.
