POST /v1/work/prices takes an optional `expected_price` (`price_micros` and `since_ms` of the price being replaced, or `{"none": true}` for a first price). When what is in effect when the new one starts differs, the tracker answers 409 `price_changed` and writes nothing, so two people changing the same price cannot overwrite each other unseen. Without the field nothing is checked. The dashboard sends the price it showed, or none, and asks for a review on a conflict, and `kbl-tool prices import --update` names the price it replaces, or none for a new one.
