POST /v1/work/prices takes an optional `expected_price` (`price_micros` and `since_ms` of the price being replaced). When another price is in effect when the new one starts, the tracker answers 409 `price_changed` and writes nothing, so two people changing the same price cannot overwrite each other unseen. Without the field nothing is checked. The dashboard sends the price it showed and asks for a review on a conflict, and `kbl-tool prices import --update` names the price it replaces.
