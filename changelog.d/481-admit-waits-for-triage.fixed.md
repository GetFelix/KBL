An admitted root is no longer handed out as fix work while its triage and augment items are open: admit mode leaves the root in Triage until a person, or the triage policy, accepts it, so `work_next` and `work_peek` show triage and augment first. The intake rule's tier no longer sets an admitted root's tier; the accept takes triage's. From format 25, finishing a fix cancels triage and augment items nobody holds and that have no recorded work instead of refusing with `open_children`, and the refusal for any other open child names it and who holds it.
