A failed check on an agent's pull request becomes `fix_check` work (format 23). Ingest reports a check that stays red after any flake re-run as `check_failed`, with the end of the job's log; the tracker opens a `fix_check` child under the root that an agent claims with the log in its context and pushes to the same request. Green checks close it. After `KBL_AUTO_FIX_TRIES` (default 1) tries on the same check, the failure goes to Needs you as before, with every try's log excerpt on the root. Reading job logs needs Actions: read. New golden logs: `core/tests/work_format_23.jsonl` and `wasm/golden/format_23`.
