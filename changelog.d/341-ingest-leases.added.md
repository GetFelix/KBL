Ingest source leases: `PUT /v1/ingest/leases` renews a worker's leases and takes the sources rendezvous hashing gives it once they are released or past their 90 s expiry, raising the epoch on each take. A worker with `tracker.group` set holds the config long poll on its own task, renews every 30 s, polls only the sources it holds, and makes no upstream call once its last successful renew is 60 s old. The config answer lists the worker's assigned sources and leases when it names its `instance`.
