The dashboard has a Spend dialog (sidebar button, or `Show spend` in the palette) listing what claims cost per root, reviewer tier and repo, largest first, and a By model table with each model's unit, usage and cost, from the fold's `spend_json()`. A model with no price shows as unpriced with its usage, never as $0. It says the total is a lower bound: lapsed claims and unpriced models are charged only the cost floor, and reported usage is the agent's own word. No new record and no format raise.
