Recording a merge (`merge_marked`) is refused while upstream shows the request open, and the item keeps who recorded it and at which offset (`merge_marked` on the item, shown as marked merged by that person). If upstream later shows that request open or closed unmerged, the item reopens into Needs you as `merge_disputed`; an upstream merge or a reopen clears the mark. A review ask made while upstream shows changes requested now lasts until the review state changes, not until the same state repeats, and a new attempt drops any ask and send-back. The wasm golden `format_11` changes: its state now carries `merge_marked` on the item a person marked merged.
