Teams, third step (#185). With `KBL_ORG_NAMESPACE=kbl` the tracker reads `org.events`, adopts `KBL_NAMESPACE` as the first team when the log is empty, and serves every team the log lists. `kbl-tool org init` creates the org namespace, `org.events` and the tracker's Felix role; `kbl-tool team create` creates a team's namespace and roles and calls the new `POST /v1/org/teams`, which starts serving the team with no restart (`GET /v1/org/teams` lists them). Org routes take a token-file person, who is an org admin. A team that loses its lease, or whose log has a record of a newer format, answers 503 with `team_unavailable` and is started again in the background while the others serve (#195).
