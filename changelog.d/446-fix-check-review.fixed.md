`fix_check` follow-ups: ingest streams a job log through GitHub's redirect into a 4 KiB tail (bytes, any content type, capped at 32 MiB) and retries a refused `check_failed` such as one sent before the raise to format 23. A held `fix_check` closes when its agent lets go after the checks went green, a red on another check no longer closes a fix, and a refused `check_failed` changes nothing.
