`fix_check` follow-ups: ingest streams a job log through GitHub's redirect into a 4 KiB tail (bytes, any content type, capped at 32 MiB) and follows only an https redirect, and retries a `check_failed` refused with `format` (sent before the raise to 23). A held `fix_check` closes when its agent lets go after the checks went green, a red on another check no longer closes a fix, and a refused `check_failed` changes nothing.
