A tracker no longer hands an agent back a claim whose hold another writer already released, as after a broker failover. Before a resumed `work_next` or an `extend` it reads the log from the last record it folded and rebuilds when anything landed after it, the log ends before it, or a different record stands in its place (a cut end refilled to the same length), so two agents cannot work one attempt. Each `work.events` record the tracker writes now names its writer in an optional `writer` field, which older readers ignore.
