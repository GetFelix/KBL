A direct claim survives a tracker restart (format 15). A `claimed` record now says `direct: true` when the tracker handed the item over from its state, as it does for a filtered claim or one among several roots. Before, a restart could lapse such a hold while its agent was still working, and a second agent could claim the item. Raise the format to 15 after upgrading (docs/upgrades.md); the golden `wasm/golden/format_15.jsonl` and `wasm/golden/format_15.state.json` pin the new fold.
