The tracker keeps its fold, writer lease and Felix connections per team behind a team table, the second step of teams (#185). Every work and ingest route is also served under `/v1/teams/{team}/`, and must name a team the token belongs to (`404` with `unknown_team` otherwise). `GET /v1/me` answers a person's teams and role in each. Each team takes at most `KBL_TEAM_INFLIGHT` calls at once (64), and a team that loses its lease answers 503 alone. There is one team, `KBL_NAMESPACE`, with the token file's callers.
