Redaction now cuts private keys whose header and `END` line are URL-encoded (`-----BEGIN%20PRIVATE%20KEY-----`, `-----BEGIN+PRIVATE+KEY-----`), including one pasted on one line with `%20` between its runs; key bodies in JSON that escape `/` as `\/` or write `+`, `/` and `=` as `+`, `/` and `=`; and PGP keys with more than 16 armor lines before the body. An armor line whose value starts another key header now ends the armor, and the redaction reads that header as a key of its own. Records already on the log are unchanged.
