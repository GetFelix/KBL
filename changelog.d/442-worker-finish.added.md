Hook workers can say they are done (format 24). A hosted worker calls the `worker_finish` tool, or `POST /v1/work/workers/finish` with its own token; the tracker logs `worker_finished`, ends the worker and gives back what it holds. `supervise_status` then lists it under `finished`, drops it from the pool and the top-up replaces it.
