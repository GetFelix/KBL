The tracker refuses to start when `KBL_NAMESPACE` is `kbl` or does not follow the key rule, since the namespace is now a team's name. A team over `KBL_TEAM_INFLIGHT` calls in flight (64 by default, and a waiting `work_next` counts) answers `503` with `team_busy` and `Retry-After: 1`; raise it for a team with more agents.
