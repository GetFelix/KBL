The tracker serves trust numbers from the summary marks people write: `GET /v1/work/metrics/summary-trust?days=` (a person only, 1 to 366 days, default 30) counts the marks written in the window, how many were accurate, and the finished attempts with no mark. An attempt counts once, by its latest mark, and the numbers name no agent, model or reviewer. The dashboard's new Metrics dialog shows them with the counts first, and says so when fewer than 10 marks are too few to read a trend from. The fold keeps each mark's time outside its wire form, so snapshots are rebuilt once (the fold revision is now 4). The summary mark paragraph in docs/decision-replay.md is true again.
