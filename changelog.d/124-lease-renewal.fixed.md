The tracker renews its writer lease on its own timer, so a sweep stuck on a slow Felix call no longer lets the lease run out, and each lease put or read gives up after 3 seconds instead of 10. A renewal refused while the lease entry cannot be read no longer fences the tracker. An extend that Felix refuses as `stale_claim` now answers `lost` only after the tracker confirms it still holds the lease; otherwise it answers 503, since a broker whose clock runs fast may have let another tracker take over the claim.
