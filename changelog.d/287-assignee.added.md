An item can have an assignee. A person takes an item (`POST /v1/work/take`), assigns it to a person of the team or to nobody (`POST /v1/work/reassign`, `{item, to}`, `not_member` for someone outside the team), and sends a note to the agent holding it (`POST /v1/work/steer`, `{item, attempt, text}`), with new records `taken`, `reassigned` and `steered`. They are format 12: the tracker refuses them until a person raises each team's log to 12 (`format_raised`, see docs/upgrades.md), after every tracker and dashboard page reading that log is this build or newer. A person assignee keeps an item out of Ready: where it would be Ready it waits in Needs you as `taken`, and an agent already holding it keeps the hold. An agent assignee is refused as `bad_assignee` for now (#324).
My queue is per person: `GET /v1/work/queue` leaves out items assigned to someone else, and each entry, and each `GET /v1/work/items` row, names its `assignee`. `kbl-mcp person` adds `take_item`, `reassign_item` and `steer_agent`. The wasm rows carry `assignee` and `held_attempt`, and the golden `format_12` pins the new records and the state they fold to.
