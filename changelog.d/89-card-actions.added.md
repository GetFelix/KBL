A person can retry, snooze, park, unpark and unblock an item over REST: `POST /v1/work/retry`, `/snooze` (`{item, until_ms}`), `/park`, `/unpark` and `/unblock` (`{item, to}`), with new records `retried`, `snoozed`, `parked` and `unblocked`. They are format 10: the tracker refuses them until a person raises each team's log to 10 (`format_raised`, see docs/upgrades.md), after every tracker and dashboard page reading that log is this build or newer. A snoozed or parked item leaves `GET /v1/work/queue` while the wait it was hidden for continues.
