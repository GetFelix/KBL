The dashboard can redo an item from a decision. The Story section has a Redo from here button on each decision (also `R` and the command palette). A confirm says that a new attempt branches from that decision, that it counts against the item's redos and how many are left, and takes an optional note, required while the pull request has a failed check. The new attempt is listed with its fork point. The tracker gains `POST /v1/work/redo` and `kbl-mcp`'s person tools gain `redo_from_decision`, both writing the existing `forked_from` record, and the dashboard's item rows carry `redos_left` and `redo_pending`. No record or format changes.
