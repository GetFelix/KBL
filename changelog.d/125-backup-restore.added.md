`kbl-tool work export <file>` writes `work.events` to a checksummed backup, `work import <file>` loads one into an empty log or carries on after the start of the same backup, and `work digest` prints a digest of the log's fold (`--backup` digests only what the file covers, `--state` also writes its canonical text). `org export`, `import` and `digest` do the same for the organization's log, so a team-mode tracker restores with its teams and token hashes. A Raft log's blank offsets are kept and restored. Backup files are readable by their owner only. CI backs up a filled team-mode stack, restores into a fresh one and compares the logs. See docs/backup-restore.md.
